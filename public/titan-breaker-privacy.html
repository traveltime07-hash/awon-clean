Titan Breaker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Publisher: PioSky Studio

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Titan Breaker (package name pl.piosky.titanbreaker) handles information.

Information handled by the game

Titan Breaker does not require an account and the publisher does not directly collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, payment information or game files from players. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the player's device.

Advertising and automatically collected data

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Google may automatically collect and share:

  • the device IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • user product interactions, including app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, service delivery and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Google acts under its own terms and privacy policy. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Advertising choices

Where required, the game presents Google's consent and privacy options. Players can also reset or delete the Android advertising ID and manage ad privacy settings in their Android device settings.

Data sharing and sale

The publisher does not sell personal data. Information described above may be processed by Google through the advertising SDK for the purposes stated in this policy.

Children

Titan Breaker is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. The publisher does not maintain player accounts or a separate player database. Requests or questions concerning privacy may be sent to the contact address below. Data processed by Google is handled according to Google's retention and deletion controls.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game, advertising services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
