Titan Breaker — Privacy Policy

Updated: 27 October 2026

Publisher and data controller: PioSky Studio / Sławomir Piórkowski. This policy covers the Android game Titan Breaker (pl.piosky.titanbreaker). Contact: traveltime07@gmail.com.

Accounts, progress and ranking

You can play as a guest with progress stored on your device. Optional sign-in uses Google and Supabase Auth. Authentication may involve your Google email, account identifiers and authentication data. We do not receive your Google password.

We process your player identifier, nickname, scores, stars, campaign progress, coin balances, lives, daily rewards, bonuses and owned upgrades or cosmetics to provide account features, backup, recovery and purchases. Signed-in progress is stored using Supabase. The in-game policy identifies the game-data storage region as the EU. Signing in with the same account allows you to restore synchronized progress on another device. Offline changes that have not synchronized may be lost if you uninstall the game.

Your nickname and scores are visible to other players in the leaderboard. Your Google email is not displayed there.

Diagnostics and support

Game and rewarded-ad diagnostic events may be associated with signed-in accounts to investigate errors, reliability and reward delivery. If you contact us, we use your email address, message and information you provide to answer your request and verify account ownership when necessary. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or purchase tokens by email.

Purchases

Google Play processes payments. Our server processes purchase tokens, product identifiers and transaction status, including refunds, to verify purchases, deliver purchased items and prevent fraud. We do not receive your payment-card details. Deleting a game account does not automatically request a Google Play refund.

Advertising and consent

Rewarded advertisements are optional. The game uses Google AdMob and Google's consent services. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad views, diagnostic and performance data for ad delivery, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. Personalization depends on applicable consent and settings.

Where required, the game presents consent choices. You can revisit available choices through the in-game advertising privacy options and manage your advertising ID in Android settings. We do not access your contacts or precise device location.

Service providers and sharing

Google provides sign-in, payment and advertising services. Supabase provides authentication and game-data hosting. These providers process information needed for their services. Public leaderboard information is described above. We do not sell personal data. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users and investigate abuse.

Provider processing may involve countries outside your own, including outside the EEA. Their applicable privacy terms explain international transfers and safeguards: Google Privacy Policy · Supabase Privacy Policy.

Purposes, choices and security

Account and purchase data are used to provide the services you request. Security, fraud prevention and necessary diagnostics support our legitimate interests in operating a reliable game. Consent is used where required for advertising technologies; legal obligations may also require certain processing. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

Online game-service connections use HTTPS to protect data in transit. Access to account services requires authentication. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and account deletion

Account saves are retained while your account is active. You can request deletion in the game under Settings → Account deletion. The request covers your player profile, cloud saves and leaderboard records and is processed within 30 days. Purchase and fraud-prevention records may be retained where required by law. Data processed separately by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion controls.

Request deletion without the game

Email traveltime07@gmail.com with the subject Titan Breaker account deletion. Include your player nickname and Google sign-in email. We may ask for information needed to verify ownership; an account is not deleted based on an email address alone. Do not send your password or purchase tokens.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes local data but does not delete your online account. Deletion of cloud progress may prevent subsequent recovery.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to certain processing. Contact us at the address above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Age group

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided account data, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

We may update this policy when game features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
